01 : PROBLEM

The problem in one picture

Empirical failure modes in urban residential charging and inter-city corridors

[ Node 01 ] Origin

Apartment

No dedicated charger

  • - Shared garages lack dedicated sub-metered 240V circuits
  • - Level 1 120V outlet adds only ~4 miles range per hour
  • - Full 60 kWh charge requires 40+ hours on standard outlets
42% of urban EV intenders cannot install home chargers.
[ Node 02 ] Constraint

EV OWNER

Needs routine charging

  • - Requires predictable weekly schedule (2-3 sessions)
  • - Loses 3 to 4 hours per week diverted to public plazas
  • - Evening battery depletion limits next-day mobility readiness
Dilemma: Vehicle ownership utility impaired by routine friction.
[ Node 03 ] Bottleneck

Public charger

Travel + wait + uncertainty

  • - 15% to 25% average dispenser offline / faulted rate
  • - Unpredictable queues during peak hours (20-45 min wait)
  • - Escalating demand charges and idle parking penalties
Result: High variance, queue anxiety, lost productive time.

The second problem: long-distance EV travel can have charging gaps, while many private chargers sit unused for hours.

Opportunity: connect convenience with underutilized charging capacity.

Critical Design Decision

A highway traveller should not need a company driver at every residential charger. The system therefore supports secure self-service.

Zero Labor Overhead: Company drivers in rural or interstate corridors are cost-prohibitive. Self-service enables unassisted operations around the clock.
Host Privacy and Security: Smart IoT locks and virtual boundaries eliminate the need for hosts to greet visitors, monitor cords, or unlock gates.
Hardware Kit: External controller retrofits onto existing NEMA 14-50 or hardwired J1772/NACS chargers in under 30 minutes without voiding EVSE UL certification.
05 : TRUST

Security: a charger is not an open door

Multi-layer virtual perimeter and optical monitoring architecture

GREEN : SAFE ZONE
Authorized charging area

Designated vehicle stall (2.5m x 5m) and EVSE pedestal. Vehicle driver is permitted to park, plug in, and remain in vehicle.

Active: Optical tracking, ground loop detector.
YELLOW : WARNING ZONE
Warning + continued monitoring

Perimeter buffer zone (3m radius). Passing pedestrians trigger a polite audio chime advising of active monitoring.

Active: Dual-beam PIR sensors, high-res clip logger.
RED : RESTRICTED ZONE
Alarm + notification + event log

Private residence entryway, side gate, or windows. Unauthorized breach triggers strobe, mobile push, and EVSE power cutoff.

Active: 100ms relay kill, police dispatch log.
CCTV + virtual perimeter monitoring keeps the charging interaction controlled. The zones are a conceptual safety framework designed to reduce unwanted movement and false alarms.

OWNER MODE Host Override
Owner authenticates -> uses charger -> session ends -> lock

The property owner retains perpetual master control. One-tap NFC or registered smartphone proximity bypasses network clearing, allowing personal charging with unmetered utility rates.

TRAVELLER MODE Guest Access
Booking creates temporary authorization -> secure session -> lock

Platform issues an encrypted, time-stamped authorization token valid exclusively for the reserved window. Connector automatically locks when unplugged from the car.

Safety: the external module is a security/monitoring controller. High-current charging should remain under a properly rated EVSE and supported control mechanisms.
07 : VERIFICATION

Every session leaves evidence

Multi-point photographic inspection and automated diff analysis

[ 01 : BEFORE ]

Pre-Session Baseline

Driver captures 4 required high-resolution inspection angles before connector release:

  • - Vehicle charge port flap and exterior body
  • - EVSE connector handle and contact pins
  • - Cable sheath continuity and strain relief
  • - Driveway parking bay surface condition
[ 02 : CHARGING ]

Telemetry and Visual Stream

Continuous background audit logging throughout the session:

  • - Voltage, current, temperature recorded at 1 Hz
  • - Optical CCTV motion clip indexing
  • - Control Pilot state transition timestamps
  • - Cryptographic session hash in ledger
[ 03 : AFTER ]

Post-Session Verification

Repeat photographs captured at identical perspectives:

  • - Re-docked connector in locked holster
  • - Charge port flap closed confirmation
  • - Cable neatly coiled on pedestal
  • - Automated damage diff verification

AI / computer vision: flags possible visible differences.

Human/technical verification: determines what actually happened and supports liability decisions.
