01 • PROBLEM

The problem in one picture

The fundamental disconnect in urban EV ownership and highway long-distance travel

■ Apartment

No Dedicated Charger

Most multi-unit residential apartment dwellers lack dedicated electrical infrastructure or dedicated garage charging plugs.

Pain Point: 42% of urban EV intenders cannot install a Level 2 home charger.
■ EV OWNER

Needs Routine Charging

Requires predictable, friction-free daily charging without losing 2 to 3 hours every week sitting at crowded public charging hubs.

Dilemma: Demands high convenience but faces daily logistical overhead.
■ Public Charger

Travel + Wait + Uncertainty

Queue times, unreliable dispenser up-time, parking fees, and lack of real-time reservation certainty degrade the ownership experience.

Friction: Broken plugs and 30-45 minute charging queues are common.

The second problem: Long-distance EV travel can have charging gaps, while many private chargers sit unused for hours.

💡 Opportunity: Connect convenience with underutilized charging capacity.

OWNER MODE

Host Priority
Owner authenticates → uses charger → session ends → lock

The property owner retains perpetual master control. One-tap NFC or Bluetooth unlock allows the host to charge their personal EV without network fees or escrow reservations.

TRAVELLER MODE

Secured Self-Service
Booking creates temporary authorization → secure session → lock

Traveller receives an encrypted time-stamped token only during confirmed booking windows. The lock automatically re-engages the moment the cable is unholstered or the time expires.

Safety: The external module is a security/monitoring controller. High-current charging should remain under a properly rated EVSE and supported control mechanisms.
