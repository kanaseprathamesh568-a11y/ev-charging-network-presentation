Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how the EV Charging Network ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, and protects personal data, vehicle telemetry, and sensor records gathered through our company-owned urban charging hubs, valet operations, and distributed highway host network.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information necessary to operate, authenticate, and secure charging sessions:

  • Identity and Contact Data: Name, verified mobile phone number, email address, and cryptographic public key tokens.
  • Vehicle Identification Data: Vehicle make, model, license plate number, battery capacity, and charge port specification.
  • Telemetry and Electrical Data: Real-time voltage, current, power draw, kilowatt-hours delivered, connector temperature, and fault status codes.
  • Perimeter and Security Data: Timestamped CCTV verification images captured before and after charging sessions, and virtual perimeter motion events within designated charging zones.
  • Geofencing Data: Precise GPS coordinates during session initiation to confirm proximity to the designated charging stall.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

Your information is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Facilitating scheduled urban valet pickup, charging, and vehicle return.
  • Generating time-bound cryptographic access tokens for distributed host chargers.
  • Verifying pre-session and post-session vehicle condition to protect both hosts and drivers against false damage claims.
  • Real-time electrical safety monitoring to prevent equipment degradation, thermal runaway, and electrical faults.
  • Processing billing, host commission disbursements, and settlement escrow.

4. CCTV and Visual Perimeter Evidence

Closed-circuit visual monitoring and photographic condition scans are conducted exclusively within the designated charging perimeter (Safe Zone and Warning Zone buffer). Visual recordings are encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Routine session footage without incident is retained for a maximum of 30 days, after which it is permanently purged. Records subject to an active dispute or liability review are retained until resolution.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Data is shared exclusively with:

  • Charger Hosts: Limited to vehicle model and booking schedule; no personal residential contact details are exchanged.
  • Certified Payment Processors: For transaction settlement under PCI-DSS compliance.
  • Emergency and Safety Authorities: If required by law or in the event of an acute electrical or physical safety breach.

6. Data Security and Cryptographic Storage

All communications between mobile applications, IoT smart lock controllers, EVSE monitors, and cloud infrastructure utilize TLS 1.3 encryption. Hardware access tokens utilize asymmetric elliptic curve cryptography with automatic expiration.

7. User Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal charging logs, request correction of inaccurate records, and request account deletion subject to legal retention obligations for financial audit records.

8. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries or technical questions regarding telemetry retention, contact the Data Protection Officer at: privacy@evchargingnetwork.internal
